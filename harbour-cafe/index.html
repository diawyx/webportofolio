Our Story

A little café with a big heart.

Founded in 2018 by Sarah and Tom, The Harbour Café started as a simple dream: a place where Bristol folk could start their day right. What began as a tiny kiosk on the waterfront has grown into a beloved neighbourhood spot.

We source our beans from independent roasters, our eggs from a local farm just 12 miles away, and our sourdough from the bakery down the road. Everything else? Made from scratch, with love, every single morning.
