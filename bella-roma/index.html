Our Story

A Taste of La Dolce Vita

Founded in 1987 by the Ricci family, Trattoria Bella Roma has been serving the heart of Covent Garden with recipes passed down through three generations. Our kitchen remains faithful to the traditions of Rome — every pasta made fresh each morning, every sauce slow-simmered with patience.

We believe that great food is more than sustenance — it is memory, warmth, and love rendered edible. Come as a stranger, leave as family.
